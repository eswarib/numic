NUMIC requirements / Cross-cutting

Security & compliance

Protect patient data and make NUMIC safe and lawful to use in an NHS setting. This covers technical controls such as encryption and hosting, and the governance documents trusts and regulators expect: clinical safety under DCB0129 and DCB0160, data protection, and the software-as-a-medical-device route. Most of this must be in place before a clinical pilot, not after.

Scope

In scope

  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • UK hosting and access control
  • Dependency scanning, SBOM and penetration testing
  • DCB0129 hazard log and clinical safety case
  • Support for trusts' DCB0160 work
  • DPIA, NHS DSPT, data minimisation, retention and deletion
  • Device classification and lifecycle standards (ISO 14971, IEC 62304)

Out of scope

  • Sign-in and roles (IAM, though SEC sets the policy)
  • Audit trail mechanics (LOG)
  • The trust's own DCB0160 sign-off

Users, data and interfaces

Users and callers

  • Clinical safety officer
  • Trust information governance and data protection officers
  • Trust IT security
  • Quality and regulatory advisers
  • NUMIC engineering team

Data it owns

  • Hazard log and clinical safety case
  • DPIA and data-flow map
  • SBOM and vulnerability reports
  • Penetration test reports
  • Retention and deletion policy

Interfaces

  • CI pipeline checks (dependency scan, secret scan, SBOM generation)
  • Documents supplied to trusts: safety case, DPIA template, DSPT evidence

SEC-F01MustAll traffic uses TLS 1.2 or later, and plain HTTP redirects to HTTPS.An external TLS scan of the deployment shows no protocol older than TLS 1.2.
SEC-F02MustData is encrypted at rest, including database storage, backups and exported files held by NUMIC.Hosting configuration shows encryption enabled for the database and backup storage.
SEC-F03MustClinical deployments are hosted in the UK.Hosting region recorded in the DPIA and confirmed in the provider settings.
SEC-F04MustAccess follows least privilege: separate database roles for the app, migrations and analytics, and named engineer access only.The app's database role cannot alter tables; the analytics role reads only the pseudonymised extract.
SEC-F05MustDependencies are scanned for known vulnerabilities in CI, and an SBOM is produced for each release.Each release has an SBOM file and a scan report with no unresolved critical findings.
SEC-F06MustAn independent penetration test is completed before the first clinical pilot, with high findings fixed.Test report and fix evidence are filed before pilot go-live.
SEC-F07MustNUMIC follows DCB0129: a named clinical safety officer, a hazard log and a clinical safety case for each release.The hazard log covers at least wrong input, wrong rule revision, wrong age calculation, misread band and missed escalation, each with a mitigation.
SEC-F08MustNUMIC supplies the information a deploying trust needs for its own DCB0160 assessment.A deployment pack with the safety case summary, intended use and known limitations exists for each pilot.
SEC-F09MustA DPIA is completed before any real patient data is stored, and NUMIC meets the NHS Data Security and Protection Toolkit standard expected of suppliers.Signed DPIA and DSPT status are on file before pilot go-live.
SEC-F10MustData collected is kept to the minimum needed. Patient names are optional, and analysis extracts are pseudonymised.A patient can be created with only MRN, date of birth and gestational age; the analysis extract contains no names, MRN or date of birth.
SEC-F11MustA retention and deletion policy sets how long each record type is kept and how it is deleted.A scheduled job deletes or archives records past their retention period; deletions are audited.
SEC-F12MustThe intended use statement, risk management (ISO 14971) and software lifecycle (IEC 62304) are documented. UKCA or MDR classification as software as a medical device is decided with regulatory advisers.Intended use and device classification decision are recorded before claims are made to clinicians outside the pilot.
SEC-F13MustThe public demo never holds real patient data and says so clearly.Demo pages show a 'synthetic data only' notice and send no data to a server.
SEC-F14ShouldThe application is built to OWASP ASVS level 2.An ASVS L2 checklist is completed for each major release.

Open questions

  • Device classification under UKCA or EU MDR, and whether NUMIC counts as software as a medical device at all.
  • Who acts as clinical safety officer for the first pilot.
  • Retention periods per record type for each trust.
  • Whether editable thresholds (admin rule editor) need limits set by validated ranges, and a predetermined change control plan.
