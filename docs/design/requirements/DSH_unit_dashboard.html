NUMIC requirements / Front end

Unit dashboard

The unit dashboard gives the ward team a single view of all active babies being followed for PHVD in their unit. It shows each baby's latest band, whether it went up or down since the previous scan, when they were last scanned and whether a scan is overdue under the unit's surveillance intervals. It supports ward rounds and handover and links through to each baby in the clinician app.

Scope

In scope

  • List of active babies per unit
  • Latest band and change since previous scan
  • Overdue-scan and pending-decision flags
  • Sorting, filtering and refresh
  • Unit summary counts

Out of scope

  • Entering scans or decisions (CLN)
  • Editing surveillance intervals (ADM)
  • Cross-unit or national benchmarking (EXT)

Users, data and interfaces

Users and callers

  • Consultant neonatologists and registrars on ward rounds
  • Nurse in charge at handover
  • Sonographers planning the scan list

Data it owns

  • None. Reads patients, latest results, decisions and unit settings.

Interfaces

  • GET /api/v1/units/{id}/patients?status=active with latest result summary
  • GET /api/v1/units/{id}/settings for surveillance intervals and band wording

Functional requirements

IDPriorityRequirementAcceptance criterion
DSH-F01MustList every active baby in the units the user has access to.A user scoped to Unit A never sees Unit B babies; discharged babies are excluded by default.
DSH-F02MustShow for each baby: identifier, age today (weeks + days), latest band, band change since the previous scan (up, down, same), last scan date and rule-set revision.A baby whose band went from low to moderate shows an 'up' marker and both bands on hover or in the row.
DSH-F03MustFlag a scan as overdue when the time since the last scan exceeds the unit's surveillance interval for the baby's current band.Proposed default from El-Dib 2020: low band at least twice weekly until stable for 2 weeks; intervals are configurable per unit and per band in ADM.
DSH-F04MustFlag babies whose latest result has no recorded clinician decision, when decision capture is enabled.With capture on, a result with no decision shows 'decision pending'; with capture off, the flag is not shown.
DSH-F05MustSort and filter by band, overdue status, pending decision and age.Filtering to 'high' shows only high-band babies; sorting by last scan puts the oldest first.
DSH-F06MustOpen the baby in the clinician app from the list.Selecting a row opens that baby's patient view.
DSH-F07MustRefresh automatically and on demand.Proposed: auto refresh every 60 s, and a refresh control; the time of the last refresh is shown.
DSH-F08ShouldShow summary counts per band and the number of overdue scans for the unit.Counts match the rows in the list after any filter is cleared.
DSH-F09ShouldShow a 'babies outside chart range' indicator where the latest scan could not be scored.A baby with an out-of-range latest scan shows that state instead of a band.
DSH-F10CouldProvide a handover view of the list suitable for printing or export.Uses the RPT unit handover summary.
DSH-F11LaterNotify staff when a baby's band rises or a scan becomes overdue.Delivered through EXT notifications once agreed with the unit.
