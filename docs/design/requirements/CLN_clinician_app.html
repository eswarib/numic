NUMIC requirements / Front end

Clinician app

The clinician app is the screen a neonatologist or sonographer uses for one baby. It shows who the baby is and how old they are, takes the measurements from a new cranial ultrasound, and shows the NumicFlow band with a plain explanation of what drove it. It shows the trend across scans against the reference lines of the selected rule set and, when the deployment allows it, records the band the clinician actually decided.

Code CLNLayer Front end Partly exists A single-screen demo exists in web/clinical-demo/ (manual scan entry, presets, trend chart, raw JSON breakdown). It has no login, no stored patients and still asks for VI percentile and reference values. 13 functional · 5 non-functional · 13 must

Scope

In scope

  • Patient header for one baby
  • Scan entry form
  • Result panel with band, layer breakdown and reasons
  • Trend chart across scans
  • Scan history
  • Decision capture panel (optional per deployment)

Out of scope

  • Editing rule-set values (ADM)
  • The all-babies view of a unit (DSH)
  • Creating or merging patient records (PAT, ADM)
  • Automatic measurement from images (EXT)

Users, data and interfaces

Users and callers

  • Neonatologists and neonatal registrars
  • Sonographers or radiologists entering measurements
  • Neonatal nurses viewing the current band (read-only role)

Data it owns

  • None stored locally. Unsent form drafts may be kept in the browser session only and must not contain patient identifiers in the URL.

Interfaces

  • GET /api/v1/patients/{id} and scan history
  • POST /api/v1/assessments (store measurements and score)
  • GET /api/v1/rule-sets and reference lines for the chart
  • POST /api/v1/assessments/{id}/decision
  • GET /api/v1/reports/assessments/{id} for the printable report

IDPriorityRequirementAcceptance criterion
CLN-F01MustShow a patient header with MRN, date of birth, gestational age at birth (weeks + days), age today (weeks + days) and NICU unit.For a baby born 2 Mar 2026 at 27+3 viewed on 14 Mar 2026, the header shows GA 27+3 and age today 29+1.
CLN-F02MustProvide a scan entry form for scan date and time, VI left and right, AHW, TOD (all in mm) and clinical concern (none, mild, clear).The form has no VI percentile or VI reference inputs; submitting without any required field shows an inline message naming the field.
CLN-F03MustShow the computed age at scan and the reference-line values for the selected rule set as read-only fields before the scan is submitted.Entering a scan date updates age at scan and the VI line in mm without a page reload; the fields cannot be edited.
CLN-F04MustShow the result: band, total score, the points from each layer and each metric, and the rule-set revision that produced it.After scoring, the panel shows e.g. 'Moderate, 5' with static, progression and clinical points and a revision such as numic_flow_levene@1; no raw JSON is shown.
CLN-F05MustExplain the result in plain language, including the distance of each measurement from its reference line and any minimum-band rule that raised the band.Each metric shows a sentence such as 'VI 1.8 mm above the 97th-centile line'; a raised band shows the rule that raised it.
CLN-F06MustShow a trend chart of VI, AHW and TOD against age at scan, with the reference line and the line + 4 mm for the selected rule set, and the band at each scan.With three stored scans the chart shows three points per metric on an age axis in weeks and draws the reference lines across that age range.
CLN-F07MustShow a scan history table with date, age at scan, measurements, band and revision for every stored scan.History is ordered newest first and each row opens the full result for that scan.
CLN-F08MustShow a clear message when the age at scan is outside the reference table range, instead of a score.A scan at 23+0 weeks under a table starting at 24 weeks shows 'Outside chart range' and no band.
CLN-F09MustUse band labels and suggested-action wording taken from configuration, defaulting to neutral wording.Changing the unit's band wording in ADM changes the text shown here without a code change.
CLN-F10MustShow the disclaimer that NUMIC supports, and does not replace, clinical judgement on every result.The disclaimer text is present on the result panel and the printable report.
CLN-F11ShouldProvide a decision capture panel, shown only when decision capture is enabled, to record the clinician's band, decision time, reason codes, optional notes and action taken.With NUMIC_CAPTURE_DECISIONS off the panel is hidden; with it on, saving creates a decision record linked to the result.
CLN-F12ShouldAllow the user to view the same scan under the other active rule set for comparison, without storing a second result.Switching from Levene to Brouwer redraws the reference lines and shows the alternative band labelled as a comparison.
CLN-F13CouldLet the user open the printable report for a result.The report opens in a new view and the action is written to the audit trail.

Non-functional requirements

IDPriorityRequirementAcceptance criterion
CLN-N01MustMeet WCAG 2.1 AA.Automated accessibility checks report no AA failures; band is conveyed by text as well as colour.
CLN-N02MustWork on ward desktops and tablets from 1024 px wide, and remain usable at phone width.No horizontal page scrolling at 1024 px or 400 px.
CLN-N03MustNever put patient identifiers in URLs or query strings.Navigating the app produces URLs containing only internal record IDs.
CLN-N04ShouldLoad the patient view quickly on a hospital network.Proposed: patient view interactive within 2 s at p95 on a typical ward connection.
CLN-N05ShouldAvoid alarm-style presentation for the high band.Usability review with clinicians signs off band presentation before pilot.
